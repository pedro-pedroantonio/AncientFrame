About AncientFrame

Built to make building a little easier.

AncientFrame is a free collection of practical construction calculators designed around the way framers and builders actually work.

Old craft. Modern precision.

Framing has always depended on geometry, proportion and careful measurement. AncientFrame brings those fundamentals into a clean digital workspace without asking builders to become mathematicians.

Free because useful tools should be useful.

The core experience is designed to be accessible without a subscription or account wall. The goal is simple: help someone solve the measurement problem in front of them and get back to the work.
