Questions

Answers before you start.

Is AncientFrame free?

Yes. The core website and calculators are intended to be free to access.

Can I use feet, inches and fractions?

Yes. The calculator input system is designed around feet, inches, eighths and sixteenths, with output formatted to practical fractions.

Are measurements saved online?

Saved measurements are stored locally on the device/browser. They are not automatically synced to an account.

How accurate are the calculations?

AncientFrame uses standard mathematical formulas and controlled rounding for displayed dimensions. Always verify the result against plans, material dimensions, site conditions and applicable code requirements before cutting or construction.

Can I request an improvement or report a problem?

Yes. Use the feedback page to report a calculation issue, request a feature, or suggest an improvement.
